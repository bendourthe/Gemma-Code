## Evidence-driven corrections

Fresh primary-source research changed several seeded assumptions before they became code:

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next is a 125B main model with 6B activated per token plus 51B n-gram embeddings and 4B multi-token-prediction parameters, not simply 125B total.
- Qwen's observed artifacts now include 186 GB FP8 and 163 GB GGUF paths between the 105 GB and 360 GB endpoints. Cross-platform llama.cpp support exists but is too new and defect-prone to pass admission.
- Video2X stable compatibility is tag 6.4.0 at `a96bda9b4d79616cc6b71b94e6945146b5b4d509`, not the previously reviewed unreleased `master` revision.
- Video2X accepts one processor per invocation. RIFE accepts an integer frame multiplier, not arbitrary 48/60 FPS, and Real-ESRGAN model assets do not support every parser-accepted scale.
- Video2X cancellation and exit 0 are not authoritative. Nexus must own cancellation state, terminate the isolated process tree, discard partial output, validate with ffprobe, and atomically publish.
- Nexus-Hub uses receipt states `RAN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `FAILED`, and `DECLINED`; aggregate coverage may be degraded. Workflow metadata belongs to `data/workflows.json`, not `data/bundles.json`.

The seed comparison and confirmed plan were amended only where these facts changed implementation behavior.
