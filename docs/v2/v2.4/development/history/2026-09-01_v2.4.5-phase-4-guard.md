## 2. Chronological Steps

### 2.1 Guard call site (T015)

**What happened**: `evaluate_install_guard` is untouched. Its signature, its reserve arithmetic, and its zero-free-disk branch are all unchanged, so its existing tests keep their meaning and the fix is one substituted argument rather than a rewrite of the check. `window.py` now passes `pending_download_gb(state)` instead of `state.selected_models_gb`.

That helper lives in `installed_models.py` rather than being inlined at the call site, because three places need the same number and any drift between them would put two answers to the same question on adjacent screens. It also owns the unknown-report fallback: a report with neither downloaded nor pending entries means the probe never ran (a headless `--model` override, or a page order that skips the picker), and unknown falls back to the full selection total. The safe reading of unknown is "assume nothing is present", which is exactly the pre-v2.4.5 behavior, never "assume nothing to download".

### 2.2 Footer and per-model affordability (T016)

**What happened**: Two consumers were still sizing the whole selection, and leaving either would have made the wizard contradict itself.

`disk_aware_footer.refresh` now displays the pending figure. Had it kept showing 194 GB against 201 GB free while the guard happily allowed the install, the same screen would have said both "this does not fit" and "proceed".

`InstallerState.can_select_model` subtracted the entire selection before checking the reserve, so it would have refused a model the guard would then have allowed. It now credits back `downloaded_gb` when the report is populated. The unknown-disk branch is deliberately unchanged: `free_disk_gb <= 0` still returns True so a failed probe cannot lock the user out, with the final Install-click guard as the backstop.

### 2.3 Testing and stabilization (T017)

**What happened**: Nine cases in `test_install_guard.py`. Three carry most of the weight:

- `test_field_case_passes_the_guard` - 194.4 GB selected, 176.4 GB present, 201.0 GB free, reserve 10 - the exact reproduction, now `ok=True`.
- `test_the_same_selection_was_refused_before_the_fix` pins the old behavior, asserting the `204.4 GB` message, so a regression is unambiguous rather than merely a missing pass.
- `test_a_genuinely_oversized_new_selection_still_blocks` - 500 GB pending against 201 GB free - proves the guard was fixed, not disabled. That distinction is the whole risk of this phase: a wrong relaxation lets a real out-of-space install begin and fail partway through a 70 GB download.

The rest cover the helper directly, the unknown-report fallback, an all-downloaded selection needing only the reserve, and the three `can_select_model` branches.

One existing test needed updating rather than fixing: `test_pages_qt.py::test_facts_and_models_are_separate_columns` asserted `Estimated installation time` was in the facts column. Phase 3 moved both estimates under the model list by design, so the assertion was inverted to pin the new placement in both directions - present in the models column, absent from the facts column.

## 5. Plan Discrepancies

- The plan described updating the guard call site, the footer, and `can_select_model` separately. They share one helper instead, because three independent computations of the same quantity is how they would drift apart.

---

## 6. Assumptions Made

- Crediting back `downloaded_gb` in `can_select_model` is correct even though a present-but-corrupt model would be re-downloaded. The re-download is bounded by that model's size, and the OS reserve absorbs it.
