## 2. Chronological Steps

### 2.1 Title row carries the action (T034)

**What happened**: The right cluster of the title row is now the size pill followed by `RowActions`: the delete button when the row is downloaded, the download button when it is compatible and not yet downloaded, the progress bar with Cancel while installing, Reveal for an external file. The round compatibility badge, the round downloaded badge, the `IconBadge` helper, and the centered action row under the body (star, downloaded checkmark, delete) are gone. `RowActions` no longer renders a downloaded checkmark beside delete, and returns nothing for an over-budget row; the incompatibility note under the name row and the group heading carry the reason.

### 2.2 Favorites leave Settings

**What happened**: The star was the only Settings control for a favorite, and the operator asked for its removal. The `favorites` state, `toggleFavorite`, `isTaskKey`, `TASK_TABS`, and the `selectionPolicy` favorite imports are removed from `ModelsSettings.tsx`. Favorites still exist: each picker writes `writeFavorite` when the user changes model, and `resolveDefaultId` still honors one. Recorded as DF-4 in known gaps because the operator has lost the one place a favorite could be cleared.

### 2.3 Availability groups on every tab (T034)

**What happened**: The list previously showed group headings only when a tab held more than one bucket and named the middle one `Available to download`. Every catalog tab now renders up to three groups in order, Downloaded, Compatible, Incompatible, each with a heading only when it has rows. The heading is a button with a chevron (`ChevronDown` open, `ChevronRight` closed), `aria-expanded`, and a row count; clicking it collapses that group's rows, per tab. `Installed outside catalog` has no compatibility data and renders one `Downloaded` group. `modelAvailabilityBucket` is unchanged; only the grouping and headings moved.

### 2.4 Incompatible cards

**What happened**: An over-budget card keeps its Phase 4 dashed border and dim tint and now also takes `opacity: 0.45`, `pointer-events: none`, and `aria-disabled`, with no action in its cluster.

### 2.5 Tests

Rewritten in `ModelsSettings.test.tsx` and `settings-models-density.test.tsx`: heading labels and counts (`Downloaded1`, `Compatible1`, `Incompatible1`), downloaded state asserted through `data-downloaded` and the presence of delete rather than a checkmark, the over-budget row having no download button and being disabled, the SANA pair (note kept, no checkmark, download only on the compatible one), the cluster order (size first, then delete) on the Gemma cards, and the absence of `models-favorite-*`, `models-actions-*`, `models-downloaded-*`, `models-compat-badge-*`, `models-downloaded-badge-*`. New: a collapse test that adds a compatible and an incompatible chat row, asserts the three headings and their order relative to the rows, collapses Compatible, and checks a single-group tab still shows its heading.
