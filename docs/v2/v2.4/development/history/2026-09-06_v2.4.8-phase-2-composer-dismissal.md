## 2. Chronological Steps

### 2.1 Shared hook (T005)

**What happened**: `desktop/src/shared/ui/useDismissOnOutside.ts` listens on `document` for `pointerdown` (and `mousedown`, which is what jsdom and some inputs deliver) and for `keydown` Escape while a surface is open. It takes a list of refs because a surface is its menu plus its toggle: a pointer on the toggle must be ignored by the hook so the button's own click handler still toggles, rather than the hook closing and the click reopening. Listeners attach only while open and detach on close or unmount.

### 2.2 Composer menus (T006)

**What happened**: `MediaComposer` gained refs on the overflow toggle, the overflow menu, the mic-menu toggle, and the mic menu, and two hook calls. The surfaces arrays and close callbacks are memoized so the effect does not re-subscribe on every keystroke in the textarea. No markup or style changed.

### 2.3 Persona popover (T007)

**What happened**: `ChatPage` gave the popover a ref and a hook call keyed on `personaOpen`. The container now uses `--bg-elevated`, `--border-subtle`, `--radius-md`, and `--shadow-md`, and is width-capped at the composer (`min(22rem, 100%)`). The label is `--fg-1` at `--text-sm`, weight 600, and is associated with the field via `htmlFor`. The textarea takes the composer field grammar: `--bg-1`, `--border-subtle`, `--radius-md`, `--font-sans`, `--text-sm`, `--fg-0`, and padding. `data-testid` values and the `setPersona` behavior are unchanged.
